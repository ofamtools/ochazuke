Reading it

Framework nouns (ochazuke.*) are kept once under the ochazuke tool; older tools carry them without the prefix and with older ochazuke versions, so a builtin verb lists every tool whose catalog carries it. Verbs a tool adds to a framework noun (a tool's ochazuke.terminal.copy) show the tool as owner.

The host's key rows are its effective rows, what config export writes (the live bindings.json included); every other tool's come from its catalog. duplicate: the same keymap, mode, keys and when twice in one tool. differs on N keymaps: one tool binds the same mode, keys and when on several keymaps to different requests, which is where a nested noun shadows its parent (over app: one of them is the app-wide keymap). Catalogs do not say which keymaps nest or are modal, so modal keymaps (chooser, confirm, palette) show up here too.

A relation is a verb with a parameter that names another noun: noun-typed (noun), source-typed (source: projects.list), or only by its name. Its mirror is a verb on the other noun relating back (the family "rich verbs" rule).
